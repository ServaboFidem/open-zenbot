The two starter design systems in this folder (default and warm-editorial) are adapted from the
design-systems/default and design-systems/warm-editorial starters of OpenDesign
(https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design, commit 59e4f2a), which is licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Changes: the text was condensed and reworded, the tokens were rewritten as a single :root block that
matches the palette in DESIGN.md, web fonts were replaced with system font stacks (the studio preview has
no network), and references to the original's own tooling were removed.
