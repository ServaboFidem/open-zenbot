# Home

This page orients you in the open-zenbot wiki. After you read it, you know what open-zenbot does, what state it is in, and where to read next. This page gives no run steps.

## What open-zenbot is

Open-zenbot is an open-source AI harness that runs on your desktop. It remembers what it learns and helps you get real work done. You bring your own keys.

A team of bots runs on your machine. Bots work in a folder you can see and use tools you choose. Bots run commands, read and write documents, call MCP servers, hand work to each other in rooms, and run routines on a schedule. Each step lands in a ledger you can read.

You stay in charge. Risky actions pause in Approvals. Bots reach only the vault and the folders you grant them. Keys and settings sit beside the vault where bots cannot reach them.

## Status

**Status: experimental.** Open-zenbot is a passion project, not a product. Expect rough edges, breaking changes, and half-built features. `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `.env.example` are the best guides.
